## What Happened

On 2026-07-24, Anthropic made Claude Opus 5 available and selected it as the default on Claude Max and the strongest option on Claude Pro.

## Why It Matters

The release translated capabilities of the larger Fable generation into a model positioned for routine professional use at lower cost. Its relevance is the changing relationship between flagship capability and routine access. The provider positioned a model below its largest specialist offering as an everyday choice for sustained professional work, including tasks involving multiple tools.

## Technical Details

Anthropic reported better cost-performance on software engineering, computer use, and knowledge-work evaluations than Opus 4.8. It also reported improvements across its life-science evaluations. These are provider evaluation findings, not independently established scientific achievements.
