## What Happened

On 2026-09-22, OpenAI expanded GPT-6 with Sol and Luna, making them available in its API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex.

## Why It Matters

The new variants spread methods behind Astra into less expensive models, making the agentic generation practical across more budgets and workloads. It also made product boundaries explicit: access through an API or an agent workspace was distinct from availability in the conversational Chat surface. That separation helps explain the rollout history of the GPT-6 family.

## Technical Details

API names were gpt-6-sol and gpt-6-luna. Standard input/output prices per million tokens were $2/$10 for Sol and $0.10/$0.50 for Luna. They were not initially available in ordinary Chat. The announcement also described improvements to context caching.
