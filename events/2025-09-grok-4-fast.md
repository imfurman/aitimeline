## What Happened

xAI introduced Grok 4 Fast as a faster and more cost-efficient model variant, positioned for general reasoning and search-heavy queries.

## Why It Matters

Fast variants often determine real-world adoption because they can be deployed more broadly and cheaply while preserving enough capability for agentic workflows.

- **Context window:** The announcement describes a very large context window for the Fast line
