## What Happened

On 2026-09-03, OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Astra and began access for a limited set of organizations, with broader availability planned afterward.

## Why It Matters

A new GPT generation combined extended computer workflows, scientific reasoning, and software engineering with additional monitoring of agent behavior. Its staged introduction is important to preserve. An announcement of a new generation and access for selected organizations does not establish that every advertised product channel already offered the model to every eligible user.

## Technical Details

OpenAI reported improvements in coding, browser use, and professional artifacts. An experimental Codex memory approach preserved notes and made earlier context windows searchable. Reported benchmark records and cybersecurity capability thresholds are OpenAI's evaluation claims, not general guarantees.
