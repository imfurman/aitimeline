## What Happened
Chinese AI developer Zhipu AI launched **GLM-5**, an advanced open-source language model with improved coding and long-running agent task capabilities.

## Why It Matters
GLM-5 signals rising competition from Chinese AI companies in foundational models, widens the global open-model ecosystem, and demonstrates significant agent-oriented capabilities.

## Technical Details
The model reportedly features enhanced performance on coding and agent tasks, more efficient inference, and scalability on domestically produced AI chips.
