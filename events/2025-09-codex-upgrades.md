## What Happened

OpenAI announced significant upgrades to Codex focused on faster, more reliable work and improved collaboration across different developer surfaces.

## Why It Matters

This kind of “tooling iteration” is crucial for staying current: an agent platform’s usefulness often depends as much on workflow integration and reliability as on raw model capability.

## Technical Details

- **Surfaces:** Described as spanning terminal, IDE, web, and mobile
- **Goal:** Better long-running, semi-autonomous coding workflows
