## What Happened

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas on September 8, 2026. The resource precomputes molecular-effect predictions for roughly nine billion possible single-letter variants in the human genome. This dates the atlas release, rather than the earlier AlphaGenome model.

## Why It Matters

Genome-wide precomputation turns a predictive model into shared scientific infrastructure. Researchers can prioritize variants and inspect potential mechanisms without independently running every prediction, helping direct limited laboratory resources toward more informative experiments.

## Technical Details

The approximately one-petabyte resource includes molecular predictions, feature attributions and sequence motifs. Its AlphaGenome Variant Impact score combines information from AlphaGenome and AlphaMissense to support variant ranking. Noncommercial research access was available through the portal and API at launch. The atlas contains model predictions, not experimental confirmation of nine billion effects. Its importance lies in supporting biological investigation and subsequent validation, rather than replacing clinical interpretation or laboratory evidence.
