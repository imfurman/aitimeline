## What Happened

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.6 as an upgrade to its Opus-class line, emphasizing improvements in coding, planning, and sustained agentic work, and introducing a 1M token context window in beta for the Opus tier.

## Why It Matters

Opus 4.6 reinforces the “long-context + sustained autonomy” frontier: the ability to reliably work inside large codebases and maintain coherence across long-running workflows is a differentiator for agentic tools.

## Technical Details

- **Context window:** 1M tokens (beta) for Opus 4.6 (per announcement)
- **Claude Code:** Announcement mentions “agent teams” capabilities in Claude Code
- **API identifier:** `claude-opus-4-6` (per announcement)
