## What Happened

Anthropic introduced Cowork as a research preview feature in Claude Desktop, positioning it as a generalized version of Claude Code’s agentic approach for non-coding tasks.

## Why It Matters

Cowork reflects a broader product convergence: “coding agents” become templates for general-purpose desktop agents that can act on local context and files, moving agentic workflows beyond engineering teams.

## Technical Details

- **Surface:** Desktop-focused research preview (as described in release notes)
- **Positioning:** Claude Code-style agentic capabilities for general work
