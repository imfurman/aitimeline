## What Happened

Google introduced Gemini 2.5, describing it as a “thinking model” designed to handle more complex problems, with the initial release framed around a Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental variant.

## Why It Matters

Gemini 2.5 is part of the broader market move toward explicit reasoning behaviors as a product capability, with “thinking” increasingly used as a user-visible and developer-configurable mode.

## Technical Details

- **Model type:** Thinking model family (per announcement framing)
- **Focus:** Reasoning improvements and stronger coding capability
