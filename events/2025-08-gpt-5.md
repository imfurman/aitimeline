## What Happened

OpenAI introduced GPT-5 as the next flagship generation, emphasizing built-in thinking and broader usefulness across tasks.

## Why It Matters

The launch continued the shift toward models that can decide when to “think longer,” reinforcing the idea that test-time compute and reasoning behaviors are core product features, not only research details.

## Technical Details

- **Positioning:** Flagship model with built-in thinking
- **Rollout:** Announced for ChatGPT and for developers via the API (separate developer-facing post)
