## What Happened

On 2026-05-19, Google launched Gemini 3.5 with the generally available Flash model across consumer applications, developer tools, and enterprise services.

## Why It Matters

A model family centered on executing long-running agent tasks became available through mass-market products as well as APIs. The same release made an agent-oriented model the default in Search AI Mode and the Gemini app. That distribution connected advances in autonomous workflows with services used well beyond specialist developer and research audiences.

## Technical Details

The release integrated with the Antigravity agent harness and supported coordinated subagents. Google reported improved coding and agent-task performance over Gemini 3.1 Pro. The announcement's future plans for Gemini 3.5 Pro were not a release of that model.
