## What Happened

OpenAI launched GPT-4.1 as a new API-focused model family (including mini and nano variants), marketed around stronger coding, better instruction following, and improved long-context usage.

## Why It Matters

GPT-4.1 made “million-token context windows” a first-class narrative for general-purpose developer models, and reframed frontier progress as a balance of quality, latency, and practical agent performance.

## Technical Details

- **Context window:** Up to 1M tokens (as described in the announcement)
- **Focus:** Coding, instruction following, long-context comprehension
- **Packaging:** Family release (flagship + smaller variants)
