## What Happened

OpenAI introduced GPT-5.3-Codex, positioning it as a more capable agentic coding model that also expands what Codex can do across computer-based professional work.

## Why It Matters

The release captures a key industry inflection point: coding agents evolving into general “computer-using” agents that combine research, tool use, and execution, while remaining steerable during long tasks.

## Technical Details

- **Focus:** Agentic coding plus broader computer-use workflows
- **Product coupling:** Closely tied to the Codex app and multi-agent supervision concepts
