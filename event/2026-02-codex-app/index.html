Codex app for macOS

OpenAI launches the Codex macOS app, positioning it as a command center for managing multiple agents and long-running tasks.

February 2, 2026 Product Launch

What Happened

OpenAI introduced a desktop Codex app for macOS, framing it as a dedicated interface for multi-agent workflows, parallel tasks, and supervision of long-running development work.

Why It Matters

As coding agents move from “single prompt → single patch” toward orchestrated, multi-thread development, a dedicated UI becomes infrastructure. This aligns with the broader trend of agent management as a first-class product surface.

Technical Details

  • Surface: Desktop app (macOS at launch)
  • Workflow emphasis: Multiple agents, parallel threads, review of diffs, and long-running tasks
