Gemma 3

Google introduces Gemma 3, a family of lightweight open models derived from Gemini research and designed for on-device or single-accelerator use.

March 12, 2025 Open Source

What Happened

Google announced Gemma 3 as a collection of lightweight open models built on the same research and technology that powers its Gemini models.

Why It Matters

Open model families like Gemma provide a bridge between frontier research and developer adoption, enabling experimentation and deployment in constrained environments.

Technical Details

  • Positioning: Lightweight, portable open models
  • Use cases: On-device and single-accelerator deployments (per announcement framing)
