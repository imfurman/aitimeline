EU AI Act Enters into Force

The European Union’s AI Act enters into force, establishing a comprehensive risk-based regulatory framework for AI systems.

August 1, 2024 Regulation

What Happened

On 1 August 2024, the EU AI Act entered into force, marking a major regulatory milestone with phased compliance obligations and enforcement structures.

Why It Matters

As frontier models and AI products proliferated, the AI Act became an influential template for risk-based governance, shaping compliance expectations for model providers, deployers, and downstream applications—particularly in high-impact domains.

Technical Details

The AI Act’s implementation is staged over time and distinguishes requirements by risk category (unacceptable, high-risk, limited risk, minimal risk), affecting both product design and deployment practices.
