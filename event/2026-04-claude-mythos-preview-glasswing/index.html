Claude Mythos Preview and Project Glasswing

Anthropic announces Mythos Preview with restricted access through the defensive-security initiative Project Glasswing.

April 7, 2026 Model Release

What Happened

On 2026-04-07, Anthropic announced Claude Mythos Preview and Project Glasswing, a coalition giving selected infrastructure organizations access for defensive cybersecurity.

Why It Matters

This was a consequential deployment decision: a highly capable general-purpose model was kept out of general availability because of its cybersecurity capabilities. The event therefore belongs in both the capability and deployment history of AI. It illustrates how access policy can become part of a frontier-model launch, rather than a separate product decision made afterward.

Technical Details

Launch partners included major cloud, software, and infrastructure organizations. Permitted work covered finding and fixing vulnerabilities in critical systems. Anthropic published technical security evaluations; its claims about discovered vulnerabilities should remain attributed to the company.
