Google Antigravity (public preview)

Google introduces Antigravity, an agentic development platform to manage coding agents across editor, terminal, and browser workflows.

November 18, 2025 Tool

What Happened

Google introduced Antigravity as an agentic development platform intended to showcase what’s possible with Gemini 3 in developer workflows.

Why It Matters

Dedicated “agent orchestration” IDE experiences are becoming a new layer of the AI stack, competing directly with specialized coding environments and agent hubs from other ecosystems.

Technical Details

  • Workflow concept: Orchestrating multiple agents across workspaces
  • Integration surfaces: Editor, terminal, and browser (per developer announcement framing)

Sources
