Mistral Large 4 Public Preview

Mistral opens a public API preview of Large 4, a trillion-parameter multimodal model trained in European datacenters.

October 6, 2026 Model Release

What Happened

Mistral opened the public API preview of Large 4 on October 6, 2026. The official announcement said downloadable weights would follow by the end of the month. As of this timeline’s October 8 cutoff, the completed event was the API preview, not an open-weight release.

Why It Matters

The preview marked a substantial increase in the scale of Mistral’s model development and demonstrated a frontier-model effort trained on the company’s European infrastructure. Its planned weight release is relevant context, but remains a future commitment at this date.

Technical Details

Large 4 is a one-trillion-parameter model with native multimodal input, targeting coding, agentic workflows and visual understanding. Mistral says it trained the model from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own datacenters. Published performance comparisons are vendor evaluations. Further architecture and post-training details were still promised alongside the future weight release.
