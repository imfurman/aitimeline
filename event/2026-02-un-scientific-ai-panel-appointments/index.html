UN Appoints Its Independent International Scientific Panel on AI

The UN General Assembly appoints 40 experts to its independent scientific panel on AI.

February 12, 2026 Milestone

What Happened

On February 12, 2026, the UN General Assembly appointed 40 members to the Independent International Scientific Panel on Artificial Intelligence. The official report was published February 13; the appointment date is confirmed in the meeting transcript. The panel itself had been legally established in 2025.

Why It Matters

The appointments put a global scientific assessment mechanism into operation. Its mandate connects research on AI capabilities, opportunities, risks and impacts with international governance discussions, providing a shared evidence base beyond individual governments and companies.

Technical Details

The appointment vote was 117 in favor, two against and two abstentions. Members serve until February 11, 2029. The panel is tasked with annual evidence-based assessments synthesizing existing research. It is a scientific advisory body, not a regulator with binding enforcement powers.
