Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash Cyber

Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and introduces Flash Cyber for trusted defenders through Fairwind.

September 2, 2026 Model Release

What Happened

On 2026-09-02, Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash for general agentic workloads and Flash Cyber for selected defensive-security users.

Why It Matters

The release combined rapid improvements in a relatively economical general model with a specialized deployment channel for vulnerability discovery and patching. The shared foundation is historically relevant: security-focused training was presented as contributing to general coding and reasoning gains, while the resulting security capabilities were deployed under a different access policy from the main model.

Technical Details

Both variants shared foundational intelligence. Flash launched at an introductory $0.75 input and $3.75 output per million tokens. Flash Cyber was available to trusted defenders through Fairwind. Google emphasized automated patching as well as vulnerability detection; it was not an unrestricted security model release.
