Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence

The Dartmouth workshop convenes, popularizing the term 'artificial intelligence' and becoming a foundational milestone for AI as a field.

June 18, 1956 Milestone

What Happened

In summer 1956, researchers gathered at Dartmouth College for the “Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence.” Notes and later reconstructions commonly place the workshop’s run roughly from June 18 through mid-August.

Why It Matters

The event is widely regarded as a symbolic “founding moment” for AI as a coherent research area, helping define a shared identity, vocabulary, and long-term ambition for the field.

Technical Details

The workshop was not a single paper or algorithm release; its importance is institutional and agenda-setting: it legitimized AI as a research program and catalyzed collaboration among early pioneers.
